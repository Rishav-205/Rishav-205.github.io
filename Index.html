Climate Change: A Threat to Our Future

Climate change is one of the major environmental issues affecting our planet today. It refers to long-term changes in temperature, weather patterns and the environment. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, cutting down forests and pollution contribute to these changes.

What Is Climate Change?

Climate change affects people, animals and the natural environment. Rising temperatures can lead to stronger weather events, changing rainfall patterns and rising sea levels. Island countries such as Fiji can be especially affected because many communities live near the coast.

Causes of Climate Change

Some major causes include burning coal, oil and gas, deforestation, pollution and activities that release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. These gases trap heat and contribute to global warming.

Effects on Our Future

Climate change can affect food production, water supplies, ecosystems and communities. In Fiji, rising sea levels and extreme weather can create challenges for coastal communities.

Think about it: Small actions can make a difference when communities work together to protect the environment.

What Can We Do?

We can help by reducing waste, saving electricity and water, planting trees, recycling and protecting our natural environment. Governments, communities and individuals all have a role to play.

Conclusion

Climate change is a serious issue that can affect our future. Understanding the problem and taking responsible action can help protect our environment for future generations.
